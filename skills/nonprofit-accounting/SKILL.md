# Not-for-Profit Accounting — ASC 958

## Modes
- **Prepare**: draft journal entries and statements from the user's facts.
- **Review**: check existing NFP accounting against the rules below.
- **Explain/FAR**: when the user says "quiz me", pose a gift scenario and grade the entries and net asset classification.

## Inputs (list what's missing)
- Gift or grant terms: the restriction and any condition.
- Dates of the promise and the payment, and the payment schedule.
- Discount rate for multi-year pledges.
- Expense detail, with its function and its natural classification.
- Any board designations.
- Endowment terms.

## Procedure
1. **Exchange or contribution?**
   - If the resource provider receives commensurate value (for example, a sponsorship with deliverables, or a fee for service), it is an exchange. Account for it under ASC 606.
   - Otherwise it is a contribution.
2. **Conditional or unconditional (ASU 2018-08)?**
   - A gift is conditional only if it contains BOTH a barrier the NFP must overcome AND a right of return or release.
   - Do not recognize a conditional gift until the barrier is met.
   - Cash received before the barrier is met is a **refundable advance** (a liability).
3. **Recognize unconditional contributions** at fair value when received or promised.
   - **With donor restrictions** for a purpose or a time period, or for perpetual endowment.
   - **Without donor restrictions** otherwise.
   - Board designations are still classified as without donor restrictions.
   - If a restriction is met in the same period the gift is received, the NFP may elect to report the gift as without restrictions. This is a consistent policy election.
4. **Pledges.**
   - Measure multi-year unconditional promises at the present value of the expected payments.
   - Accretion of the discount is recorded as contribution revenue.
   - Record an allowance for uncollectible pledges.
   - Payments due in future periods carry an implied time restriction.
5. **Releases.**
   - When a purpose or time restriction is met, record a reclassification: decrease net assets with donor restrictions and increase net assets without donor restrictions ("net assets released from restrictions").
   - All expenses are reported in net assets without donor restrictions.
   - For long-lived assets, the restriction is released when the asset is placed in service, unless the donor specifies a time period.
6. **Contributed services.** Recognize them only if either:
   - They create or enhance a nonfinancial asset, OR
   - They require specialized skills, are provided by people who have those skills, and would typically be purchased otherwise.
   - General volunteer work (envelope stuffing, for example) is not recognized.
   - Gifts-in-kind are recognized at fair value, with the disclosures required by ASU 2020-07.
7. **Agent or intermediary.**
   - If the NFP is only passing resources to a beneficiary it doesn't choose, record a liability, not revenue.
   - Exception: if the donor grants the NFP variance power, it is a contribution.
8. **Endowments.**
   - Perpetual principal is classified with donor restrictions.
   - Earnings stay restricted until appropriated, under UPMIFA.
   - Disclose underwater endowments.
   - Report investment return net of external and direct internal investment expenses.
9. **Statements.**
   - Statement of financial position, with two net asset classes.
   - Statement of activities, showing the change in each class.
   - Statement of cash flows. Contributions restricted for long-term purposes are financing inflows.
   - Analysis of expenses by function (program, management and general, fundraising) and by nature. Every NFP must present this, either as a statement or in the notes.
   - Liquidity disclosure.
   - Joint costs may be allocated to programs only if the purpose, audience, and content criteria are all met.

## Tie-outs (report PASS/FAIL)
- Net assets with donor restrictions roll forward: beginning + restricted contributions − releases = ending.
- Releases appear as an equal and opposite reclassification between the two classes.
- Total expenses by function equal total expenses by nature.

## Output
1. Classification analysis: exchange or contribution, conditional or unconditional, restricted or unrestricted.
2. Journal entries.
3. Roll-forwards for both net asset classes.
4. Statement of activities draft.
5. Functional expense matrix if needed.
6. Flags for judgment items.

## Worked examples
**Restricted gift, partly spent**
- The NFP receives 50,000 cash restricted to a youth program.
  - Dr Cash 50,000 / Cr Contribution revenue — with donor restrictions 50,000.
- It spends 30,000 on the program this year.
  - Dr Program expense 30,000 / Cr Cash 30,000.
  - Release: Dr Net assets with donor restrictions 30,000 / Cr Net assets without donor restrictions 30,000.
- End of year:
  - With donor restrictions: +50,000 − 30,000 = **+20,000**.
  - Without donor restrictions: +30,000 release − 30,000 expense = **0**.

**Matching grant**
- A funder offers 100,000 if the NFP raises 100,000 from other donors. This is conditional: there is a barrier and a right of release.
- Recognize nothing until the match is met.
- If the funder pays cash early: Dr Cash / Cr Refundable advance.

## Boundaries
Conditional vs. unconditional, exchange vs. contribution, and agent determinations are judgments. Flag them for reviewer sign-off.
